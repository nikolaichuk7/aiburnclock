# To NASCIO (state CIO association) — programs / research director

Subject: A free daily release for every state on federal AI contracts and the cost of retrieval by reading

Dear {NAME},

The AI Burn Clock publishes a dated release for each of the fifty states and the District of Columbia: federal prime contracts naming AI performed in the state (USAspending.gov), per resident, share of the U.S. total, state debt for scale (Census), and an estimate of what an AI agent reads for nothing when it searches by reading whole files, with every factor on a slider and every source linked. Releases are datelined at the state capital and free to republish.

For NASCIO members the useful part is the remedy: an open-source, on-premises index that answers "where is it" before the agent reads (16 to 47 times less read per answer in our measurement), with a pilot that fits on one laptop. I would be glad to brief a working group or write a short piece for your members, and to send any state its one-page memo.
