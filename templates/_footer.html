AI Burn Clock
An independent statistical observatory maintained by the XERJ community. Not affiliated with any government agency.

Official series are reproduced as published from the U.S. Treasury, USAspending.gov and the Census Bureau and are shown for scale. The index's own estimate is a stated scenario; every factor is a control on the page. Organisations are named only from their own public disclosures, each linked to its source. Corrections are published on the technical note with the date.
