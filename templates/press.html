What it is. A live estimate of how much money is burning in AI on reading files nobody needed, built from official sources (Treasury, USAspending, Census, Gartner) with a four-factor formula whose every factor is a slider on the page.

Why now. AI agents that search by reading pay for every file they open. On real coding tasks an index that returns the passage instead cuts tokens by 2.7x; on one production codebase we measured 16 to 47 times less read. Worldwide AI spending is forecast at $2.59 trillion in 2026, up 47 %. The habit scales with the spend.
